Personal project Fashion branding

anatomy.

A personal, fictional project for a clothing brand that's especially close to my heart. Passionate about fashion and independent creation, I drew inspiration from French indie designers to bring "anatomy." to life.

I created a full branding, an iconography and an entire graphic universe with its variations. The theme of the human body and nature allows for striking visuals with many alternatives, later applied to garments as mockups. This project let me fully express my creativity.
