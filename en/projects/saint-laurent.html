Saint Laurent — Fashion Week

This project shows my grasp of the content production chain (shooting, technical, organisation). During my time at a live production company, I worked on major projects during Paris Fashion Week, notably the production file for the Spring/Summer Saint Laurent HOMME show.

My role: video technician. The goal was to ensure a high-end multi-camera capture for international broadcast, respecting the show's artistic direction. With my team I organised the control room setup, the cabling deployment (fibre, RF) and the stage preparation.
