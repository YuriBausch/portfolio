Government of New Caledonia — Tech for Good

During my time at the Government of New Caledonia, I managed the institutional social media campaign for the Tech for Good initiative — a call for innovative projects dedicated to ecological transition and greentech.

I designed a varied series of posts and stories: visual explainers, calls for applications, and the announcement of winning projects. The goal: make public action accessible and engaging for a young, entrepreneurial audience.

  • Multi-platform campaign (Facebook, Instagram)
  • Creation of branded visual assets
  • Targeted audience engagement
